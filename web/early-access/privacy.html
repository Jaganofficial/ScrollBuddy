Privacy

The short privacy note

This page covers two things: the email you leave on this website, and what the app does on your phone.

The email you give us here

  • We ask for one thing: your email address.
  • We use it for one thing: to send you the invitation when early access opens.
  • It is kept in a private Google Sheet that only we can open.
  • We don't sell it, share it or add it to any other list.
  • To have it removed, write to contactjaganeeshwar@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

This website has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

What the app does on your phone

  • It has no internet permission, so nothing it counts can leave your phone.
  • Android tells it about two apps only, Instagram and YouTube, and about two kinds of event in them: something scrolling, and one of their screens coming to the front.
  • It cannot read what is on your screen, what you type or your passwords.
  • It keeps a count of your swipes through Reels and Shorts and the time between them, for each day, on your phone. You can clear it from the History tab, and uninstalling the app deletes everything.
  • There is no account, no advertising and no third-party code inside the app.

Who we are

Vaathi ride-uhh! is an independent app. It is not affiliated with Instagram, YouTube, Meta or Google.
